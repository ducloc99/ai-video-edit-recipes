# Prompt

Make a 17-second vertical (1080x1920) 3D reel in HyperFrames with Three.js, drawn entirely in code, with no AI images and no paid services. Show an isometric miniature diorama of a cosy tiny room in a pastel, low-poly look, like a premium Blender render. It sits on a cream-and-mint rounded slab over a soft peach background. The room builds itself piece by piece with soft spring bounces: the slab, the wooden floor planks, two walls rising, a window showing a sunset over pink hills, a rug, a bed (frame, mattress, blanket, pillow), a shelf with pastel books and a succulent, a desk with a chair, monitor, keyboard, mug and desk lamp, a framed picture, a big leafy plant by the window, a pouf, a small plant and, last, an orange cat curled up asleep on the bed. Low golden sun comes through the window and leaves a window-shaped patch on the floor, with soft shadows everywhere. Around 9 seconds the sun sinks behind the hills and the sky and background shift from peach through dusk pink to deep indigo, with stars and a crescent moon. The monitor wakes, then the warm desk lamp clicks on with one small flicker and lights the room. The camera orbits slowly and pushes in a little the whole time, the leaves sway and the cat breathes. End with a small italic "Tiny Room" title. Use calm, dreamy lo-fi synth and piano music only, with no sound effects. Keep it deterministic: seeded random, and every frame drawn from timeline time.
